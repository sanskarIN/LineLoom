# Community code of conduct

We adopt the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (attributed to the Contributor Covenant project, CC BY 4.0).

Participants should act respectfully, welcome constructive criticism and diverse perspectives, avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior, and keep discussion focused on collaborative software development. Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation where necessary. For non-sensitive conduct concerns, contact maintainers via GitHub issues; for privacy-sensitive reports, ask for a private reporting channel without exposing details. We will prioritize fair review without promising a fixed response timeline.

The Contributor Covenant's full text, interpretation and attribution are available at the linked project website.
